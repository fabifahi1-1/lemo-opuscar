# LOCATIONS

14 locations. Each one is a stack of cut paper in the lightbox, built from the director's seven layers. Colours are one flat colour per sheet, back to front: bright and translucent at the back, dark and opaque at the front (the style's value ladder). Light is warm amber from behind; each location has one accent.

## How the layers work

| Layer | Content | Parallax speed | Paper | Translucency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Sky: sky, clouds, sun or moon; clouds drift by themselves | 0.1 | lightest, coolest | 0.9 |
| L2 | Far: far hills, distant trees, far castle; lighter and softer than near layers | 0.25 | light | 0.75 |
| L3 | Middle: the main place (mill, walls, road, fields) | 0.5 | mid | 0.5 |
| L4 | Action: characters and the props they touch; soft shadow under their feet | 0.75 to 1.0 | saturated, backlit coloured card | 0.35 |
| L5 | Near ground: grass edge, floor edge, table edge, rocks | 1.2 | dark | 0.12 |
| L6 | Foreground frame: leaves, flowers, curtain, doorframe covering the left or right edge; a little soft (shallow depth of field); in about half the shots | 1.5 | near black | 0.05 |
| L7 | Atmosphere: dust in sunbeams, falling leaves, flour specks, candle dust; drifts by itself | own motion | light specks | glows |

- **Parallax is applied as an explicit slide factor per layer** on top of the box's real perspective (the box is only 0.16 m deep, so perspective alone cannot make L1 move at a tenth the speed of L6). The camera moves in screen space; each sheet follows it at its own factor.
- A wide shot uses all six layers; every shot moves at least four.
- Characters are jointed paper puppets on L4: head, neck, body, upper and lower arms, hands or paws, legs, feet, tail and ears; eyes (open, half, closed), eyebrows, mouths and a blush sheet are separate small cut pieces. L5 and L6 stay dark, so the value ladder holds even though the characters are saturated.

## Palette rules

- Puss: orange (the main accent), white paws, green eyes. Tom: warm brown tunic, then the blue jacket (the second accent, only after he changes). The king: purple and gold. Princess: soft rose. Each stays identical in every shot.
- Light is always warm amber through paper; the lit window, lantern and candle are the only saturated glows besides the accents.

## The locations

### 1. TABLE: the lantern and the storybook (opening, end screen)
Real-world set (the style's "box as keepsake"). A small paper lantern beside an open storybook on a dark wooden table; a candle-warm spill on the table.
- L1 dark room wall, L2 shelf and window dark blue `#10162e`, L3 lantern body (warm vellum, glowing) and book, L4 the open book pages that turn, L5 table edge `#2a1a12`, L6 out-of-focus dried flowers on the right edge, L7 candle dust.
- Light: the lantern, amber `#ffb862`. Sound: candle crackle, a quiet room.
- Used: shots 1, 2 and the end screen (shot 185).

### 2. MILL: the mill by the river (Part 1)
Morning. The mill house with a turning waterwheel, the river, the step, the door, and a shed corner at the side.
- L1 morning sky `#f4dcb0` with slow clouds, L2 far hills `#9db4c9`, L3 the mill `#6d5a8a` with the wheel and the river sparkle, L4 Tom, Puss, the brothers, the donkey, the father's chair and hat, L5 the step and grass `#2c2a3a`, L6 reeds on the left `#10131f`, L7 flour specks in sunbeams.
- Shed set: back wall planks `#d9c19a`, shelves, bag and boots on a hook, a window of warm light.
- Accent: Puss orange. Sound: river, waterwheel creak, birds, grinding stones.

### 3. ROAD: the country road (the strut, the run, the carriage)
- L1 pale blue sky, L2 distant fields, L3 a fence and trees, L4 the road with the walkers, L5 grass edge, L6 flowers on the left, L7 pollen and dust puffs.
- Ladder: sky `#cfe3ee`, far `#8fb08e`, mid `#5f8a5a`, front `#1d2f22`. Used for tracking shots; the layers slide at their own speeds.
- Sound: birds, wheels and hooves when the carriage rolls.

### 4. FOREST: the forest edge (berries)
- L1 morning sky `#f2e0b8`, L2 far firs, L3 berry bushes and tree trunks, L4 Puss and the bag, L5 ferns, L6 hanging leaves on the right, L7 drifting spores in a sunbeam.
- Accent: the red berries. Sound: birdsong, rustling leaves.

### 5. HILL: the hill road up to the king's castle
- L1 sky, L2 the far castle with towers and flags `#8f86b8`, L3 the hill, L4 tiny Puss, L5 rocks and grass, L6 a tree branch, L7 wind-blown petals.
- Low angle, tilt up to the towers. Sound: wind, flags.

### 6. THRONE: the king's throne room
- L2 the back wall with arched windows `#e2c58f`, L3 the throne, red curtains and pillars `#6a2d4a`, L4 the king, guard, Puss, the table of gifts, L5 the carpet edge `#2a1420`, L6 a curtain on the left `#150a10`, L7 dust in window beams.
- Window light changes in the dissolves "the next week" and "the week after that" (cooler, then golden).
- Sound: echo, a quiet room.

### 7. SQUARE: the village square and the bakery stall
- L1 clear sky, L2 rooftops, L3 houses and the crier's stand, L4 villagers, the crier, the bakery counter with Puss and the baker, L5 cobble edge, L6 bunting across the top and left, L7 flying pigeons.
- Bakery stall set: striped awning, a counter, bread and cookies. Sound: crowd murmur, bell.

### 8. RIVER: the riverbank by the road
- L1 sky, L2 far trees, L3 the river with sparkle, bank, a bush and a rock, L4 Tom, Puss, the servant, the clothes, L5 reeds, L6 grass blades on the right, L7 water drops.
- Splash set-piece: a paper splash fan that opens.

### 9. CARRIAGE: inside the royal carriage (window frame)
- L6 the carriage window frame and curtain `#2a1420`, L4 the king, princess, Tom in blue, L3 the cushioned seat, L2/L1 the passing landscape slides by (the "POV" shots look through the frame at the fields).
- Sound: wheels, hooves, creaking leather.

### 10. FIELDS: the fields, the sheep meadow, the apple orchard
- Three sub-sets in a row for the long pan: wheat fields `#e9c97a`, sheep meadow `#a7c68b`, apple orchard `#7ea36c` with red apples.
- L4 the farmers, shepherd, apple picker, sheep; L5 low fence; L6 tall grass; L7 butterflies.
- Sound: the "baaa", crunching cookies, wind in grass.

### 11. OGRE: the ogre's castle (gate, door, roof, step)
- L1 darker, stormier sky `#8d9cc4`, L2 far tower silhouettes, L3 the huge door `#4a3a6a` and the stone wall, L4 Puss, the ogre, lion, mouse, the doormat BEWARE OF OGRE, the slippers on the step, L5 stone steps, L6 a gate arch on the left, L7 mist.
- The roof is a sloped paper sheet for the slide; the door is twice as tall as a normal door.
- Accent: the pink slippers (a second accent, the only one in this place).
- Sound: low drone, thunder, tile slides.

### 12. HALL: the ogre's great hall (empty, then lived in)
- L2 tall arched windows, L3 stone pillars, L4 Puss, the slipper, Tom and the princess later, L5 the floor edge, L6 a doorframe on the right, L7 dust, then candle glow when lived in.
- Empty: cool grey-violet, echo. Later: the same set with warm candles and rugs, the proof of "the castle is theirs".

### 13. KITCHEN: the castle kitchen at night
- L1 night sky in the window `#10162e` with stars, L2 the kitchen wall, L3 cupboards and the window, L4 the table, the loaf, Tom and Puss, L5 the table edge, L6 a hanging copper pot on the left, L7 candle dust.
- Native move: the lamp turns on (the candle, then the window, then the room). Sound: crickets, candle crackle.

### 14. BEDROOM: Puss's softest bed
- L2 the wall with a round window, L3 the headboard, L4 the bed, the pillow, Puss with boots on, L5 the rug, L6 a curtain, L7 floating zzz motes.
- Sound: soft snore, a lullaby music box.
